Annotation Key
  • Template (Parental) Strands

    Both strands of the original double helix. Serve as the blueprint for new synthesis. Arrows show 3′→5′ polarity read by polymerase.

  • Leading Strand (daughter)

    Synthesized continuously 5′→3′ toward the replication fork. One uninterrupted new strand.

  • Lagging Strand (daughter)

    Synthesized discontinuously 5′→3′ away from the fork as Okazaki fragments.

  • Replication Fork

    The Y-shaped junction where helicase unwinds the double helix, exposing both template strands.

  • Phosphate Group (PO₄) ×4

    White tubes in the model. Negatively charged; links nucleotides via phosphodiester bonds to form the backbone.

  • 3′–OH Group ×4

    Free hydroxyl on the 3′ carbon of deoxyribose. DNA polymerase adds the next nucleotide here. Each free strand end has one.

  • Deoxyribose (pentagon node)

    Black pentagon connectors in the model. The 5-carbon sugar lacking the 2′–OH that distinguishes DNA from RNA.

  • Nitrogenous Base Pair

    Colored tubes bridging the two backbones. A pairs with T (2 H-bonds); G pairs with C (3 H-bonds).
