Step 1 — The Big Picture

One molecule, seven names.

Think of it as a promotion ladder. The DNA molecule gets a new title each time it puts on more "clothing" (proteins) or gets duplicated.

1a Double helix (naked) no proteins, 2 nm
Minus (-) histones ↓
1b Double helix (wrapped) ~147 bp on histones
Plus (+) Histones ↓
2 Nucleosome "beads on a string"
+ higher-order fiber organization ↓
3 Chromatin 10-nm fiber, interphase
Interphase (also as unit of inheritance) ↓
4 Chromosome (generic) all phases
Always applies ↓
5 Unreplicated chromosome 1 DNA, no sister
Anaphase separates it - S-phase replicates it → ↓
6 Replicated chromosome 2 DNA held by cohesin
S-phase replicates it - Anaphase separates it ↓
7 Sister chromatid one of the two copies
Key insight: Names 1a→3 are about protein packaging level. Names 4→7 are about replication state. Two separate dimensions, one table.
