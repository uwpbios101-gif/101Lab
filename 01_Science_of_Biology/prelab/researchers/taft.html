📘 Step-by-Step Guide

Observation — Facts only. No explanations. Describe what you see in the flow tank. Avoid "because."

Curiosity Question — Broad wonder-question. Starts with "How does…", "Is there…", "To what extent…". Not testable yet.

Research Question — Focused & testable. Names the fin property (IV) and the swimming performance measure (DV).

Hypothesis — A tentative, falsifiable answer. Proposes a specific biomechanical mechanism. Uses "will" and compares the two fish.

Prediction — If–Then format. States the measurable swimming outcome expected if the hypothesis is correct.

Variables & Controls — IV: fin stiffness or shape. DV: station-holding time, oxygen use, tail-beat frequency. Controlled: fish size, water temp, flow speed.

Experimental Design — Flow tank setup, groups, replicates, measurement methods, expected result pattern.

Theory vs. Law — Functional morphology theory vs. laws of fluid dynamics (Bernoulli, drag equations).
