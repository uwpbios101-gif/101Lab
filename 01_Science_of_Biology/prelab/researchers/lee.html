📘 Quick Reference

Observation – Just the facts. No explanations. Use your senses or instrument data.

Curiosity Question – Broad, wonder-based. Starts with "How does...", "Is there...".

Research Question – Focused, testable. Identifies independent and dependent variables.

Hypothesis – A tentative answer. Proposes cause‑effect, falsifiable.

Prediction – "If–Then" statement: what measurable outcome if hypothesis is true.

Variables & Controls – Independent (manipulated), Dependent (measured), Controlled (constant).

Experimental Design – Detailed plan, groups, replicates, expected results pattern.

Theory vs. Law – Theory: broad explanation. Law: concise math description.
