📘 Step-by-Step Guide

Observation — Facts only. No explanations. Use your senses or instrument data. Avoid "because."

Curiosity Question — Broad wonder-question. Starts with "How does…", "Is there…", "To what extent…". Not testable yet.

Research Question — Focused & testable. Names the independent variable and dependent variable.

Hypothesis — Tentative, falsifiable answer. Proposes a cause-effect mechanism. Uses "will" and includes a comparison.

Prediction — If–Then format. States the measurable outcome if the hypothesis is correct.

Variables & Controls — IV: what you manipulate. DV: what you measure. Controlled: everything kept constant.

Experimental Design — Groups, replicates, measurement methods, and both possible result patterns.

Theory vs. Law — Theory: broad, evidence-based explanation. Law: concise mathematical description.
