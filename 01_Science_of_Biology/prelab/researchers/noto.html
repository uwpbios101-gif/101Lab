📘 Step-by-Step Guide

Observation — Facts only. No explanations. Report what you see at the dig site. Avoid "because."

Curiosity Question — Broad wonder-question. Starts with "How does…", "Is there…", "To what extent…". Not testable yet.

Research Question — Focused & testable. Names the taphonomic process (IV) and the preservation outcome (DV).

Hypothesis — A tentative, falsifiable answer. Proposes a post-death mechanism. Uses "will" and includes a comparison.

Prediction — If–Then format. States the bone-level evidence expected if the hypothesis is correct.

Variables & Controls — IV: inferred post-death process. DV: bone condition (articulation, surface marks). Controlled: same rock layer, same time period.

Experimental Design — Systematic documentation, surface analysis, comparison to modern analogs.

Theory vs. Law — Taphonomic theory vs. geological laws (superposition, uniformitarianism).
