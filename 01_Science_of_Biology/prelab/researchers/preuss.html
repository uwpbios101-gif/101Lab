📘 Step-by-Step Guide

Observation — Facts only. No explanations. Use your senses or instruments. Avoid "because."

Curiosity Question — Broad wonder-question. Starts with "How does…", "Is there…", "To what extent…". Not testable yet.

Research Question — Focused & testable. Names the independent variable (what you change) and dependent variable (what you measure).

Hypothesis — A tentative, falsifiable answer. Proposes a cause-effect relationship. Uses "will" and includes a comparison.

Prediction — If–Then format. States the measurable outcome expected if the hypothesis is correct.

Variables & Controls — IV: what you manipulate. DV: what you measure. Controlled: everything kept constant.

Experimental Design — Describe groups, replicates, and the result pattern that would support or reject your hypothesis.

Theory vs. Law — Theory: broad, evidence-based explanation. Law: concise mathematical description.
